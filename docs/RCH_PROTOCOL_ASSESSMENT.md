# RCH protocol assessment

The current evidence assessment is maintained in
[RCH_PROTOCOL_FINDINGS.md](RCH_PROTOCOL_FINDINGS.md).

The essential boundary remains:

- Ethernet port 23 is documented by accessible RCH material;
- installed transport/raw/Telnet behavior, official commands, response/error
  meanings, and fiscal-success semantics are not established by that fact;
- the observed frame layout/BCC can be verified on supplied complete frames;
- business command roles and document lifecycles remain inferred;
- the new supplied corpus is one incomplete job and cannot verify four
  photographed documents.

See [RCH_SOURCES.md](RCH_SOURCES.md) for the official-source register and
[KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) before deployment.
